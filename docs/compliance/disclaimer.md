# KejaApp Disclaimer

## 1. Purpose and scope

This document expands the brief "Disclaimers" clause already in the [Terms of Service §8](terms-of-service.md#8-disclaimers) — the same pattern the [Indemnity Policy](indemnity-policy.md) followed for §10. It states plainly what KejaApp does not warrant, guarantee, or take responsibility for, and why, grounded in the same Kenyan consumer-protection limits that govern every disclaimer-type clause in these terms.

## 2. "As is," and what that does and doesn't mean

KejaApp is provided **"as is"** and **"as available."** That phrase means KejaApp doesn't promise the platform is fault-free, always available, or fit for a purpose it wasn't described as serving. It does **not** mean KejaApp can disclaim liability without limit — Kenya's **Consumer Protection Act, 2012** (§5(3)) voids any provision that negates or varies an implied condition or warranty under that Act or the Sale of Goods Act, and §13/§15 prohibit a disclaimer so one-sided it becomes an "unconscionable representation." See [Indemnity Policy §4](indemnity-policy.md#4-what-this-indemnity-does-not-do) for the fuller statutory grounding, which applies here in the same way.

## 3. No warranty on listing or profile accuracy

- **Landlords and agencies** are solely responsible for their own listing content — description, price, amenities, photos, availability. KejaApp does not inspect properties, verify ownership, or confirm that a listing is still available at the price shown.
- **Movers** are solely responsible for their business profile and their ability to actually deliver the service they describe.
- A **"Verified"** badge means KejaApp's admin team reviewed submitted business documents against its verification workflow (see [Code of Ethics §2.4](code-of-ethics.md#24-safety-and-trust)). It is a check on business identity, not an appraisal of a property, a guarantee of service quality, or a warranty that a transaction will go as expected.

## 4. No professional advice

Nothing on KejaApp — a listing, a price estimate, a mover's quoted distance/price calculation, a review, or any in-app content — is legal, financial, valuation, or professional advice. Location-based distance/price estimates for movers are computed figures, not a quote any mover is bound to honor; a tenancy's actual terms are whatever the tenant and landlord/agency agree directly, not anything KejaApp states about a listing.

## 5. User-generated content is the author's own

Reviews, inquiry messages, feedback, and listing descriptions are written by the users who submit them and reflect their own experience or claims, not KejaApp's independent verification or endorsement (see [Terms of Service §5](terms-of-service.md#5-reviews)). KejaApp does not edit review content or ratings — only the reviewed party may post a public response.

## 6. Third-party links and services

KejaApp surfaces contact details and links a user provides (a phone number, an email, a WhatsApp link) so another user can reach them directly. Following one of these links or contacting a user through it is between you and that user — KejaApp is not a party to it and doesn't warrant anything about where it leads (see the [Payment Boundary](../../README.md#payment-boundary) for the same principle applied to money specifically).

## 7. What this disclaimer does not override

Consistent with [Terms of Service §8](terms-of-service.md#8-disclaimers)'s own closing line: nothing here limits any right or protection under the Consumer Protection Act, 2012, or other Kenyan law that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited by agreement. This document narrows what KejaApp claims to guarantee — it does not, and cannot, strip a statutory right.

This document is KejaApp's honest statement of what it does and doesn't warrant, written to be genuinely useful rather than boilerplate. It is **not** legal advice, and has not been reviewed by a lawyer licensed in Kenya — see [Terms of Service §14](terms-of-service.md#14-scope-and-limitations) for the same caveat applied to the terms generally.
