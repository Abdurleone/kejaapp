# Governance and Policies

KejaApp maintains a full set of governance, legal, and data-protection documentation alongside the code, in [`docs/`](https://github.com/Abdurleone/kejaapp/tree/main/docs). It's written to align with Kenya's Constitution and Data Protection Act 2019, the GDPR, and ISO/IEC 27001-family control themes — as operational, product-grounded documentation, not a certified compliance filing. Every document says so explicitly in its own "scope and limitations" section.

For the canonical, most up-to-date version of each document, follow the links below into the repo — if anything here looks out of date, trust the linked file over this page.

## Platform ethics and terms

- **[Code of Ethics](https://github.com/Abdurleone/kejaapp/blob/main/docs/compliance/code-of-ethics.md)** — the principles governing how KejaApp is built and operated: honesty in listings, non-discrimination, privacy, safety/trust, fair dealing between roles, and admin conflict-of-interest rules. Distinct from [`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`](https://github.com/Abdurleone/kejaapp/blob/main/docs/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), which governs contributor conduct in this repo, not platform use.
- **[Terms of Service](https://github.com/Abdurleone/kejaapp/blob/main/docs/compliance/terms-of-service.md)** — the agreement formed at registration: what KejaApp is and isn't (a discovery/coordination layer, not a party to any tenancy or payment), account rules, listing-content licensing, disclaimers, liability limits, and Kenya as the governing law.
- **[Acceptable Use Policy](https://github.com/Abdurleone/kejaapp/blob/main/docs/compliance/acceptable-use-policy.md)** — the enforceable list of prohibited listing content and conduct behind the Code of Ethics, tied to the same violation/suspension/ban mechanism already built into the platform, and grounded in the criminal offences the Computer Misuse and Cybercrimes Act, 2018 attaches to several of them (harassment, security bypass, false listings/reviews, identity theft).
- **[Copyright and Takedown Policy](https://github.com/Abdurleone/kejaapp/blob/main/docs/compliance/copyright-and-takedown-policy.md)** — the notice-and-takedown process for a copyright claim against uploaded listing/profile content, grounded in the Copyright Act's 2019 online-infringement amendment (48-business-hour takedown window).
- **[Dispute Resolution & Complaints Policy](https://github.com/Abdurleone/kejaapp/blob/main/docs/compliance/dispute-resolution-policy.md)** — draws the line between disputes *between users* (which KejaApp doesn't mediate) and complaints *about KejaApp itself or an enforcement decision* (which it commits to a defined review process for).
- **[Disclaimer](https://github.com/Abdurleone/kejaapp/blob/main/docs/compliance/disclaimer.md)** — expands Terms of Service §8: what KejaApp does and doesn't warrant about listings, verification badges, user-generated content, and third-party links, within the same Consumer Protection Act limits as the Indemnity Policy.
- **[Anti-Bribery and Anti-Corruption Policy](https://github.com/Abdurleone/kejaapp/blob/main/docs/compliance/anti-bribery-and-anti-corruption-policy.md)** — zero-tolerance statement aligned with Kenya's Anti-Bribery Act, 2016 (Cap 79B): where bribery risk actually shows up in KejaApp's operations (verification review, vendors, future government dealings), the 24-hour EACC reporting duty, and whistleblower protections.
- **[Indemnity Policy](https://github.com/Abdurleone/kejaapp/blob/main/docs/compliance/indemnity-policy.md)** — expands Terms of Service §10: what users indemnify KejaApp against and why, grounded in the Law of Contract Act's guarantee/indemnity distinction and the limits the Consumer Protection Act, 2012 places on one-sided or ambiguous terms.
- **[Director and Officer Indemnification Policy](https://github.com/Abdurleone/kejaapp/blob/main/docs/compliance/director-officer-indemnification-policy.md)** — a forward-looking read of the Companies Act, 2015's rules on indemnifying directors, honestly scoped to the fact that KejaApp isn't yet a registered company with directors in that sense.

## Data protection and privacy

- **[Data Protection Policy](https://github.com/Abdurleone/kejaapp/blob/main/docs/compliance/data-protection-policy.md)** — what personal data is collected, the legal basis for each category, third-party processors, retention/deletion, subject rights, security measures, breach notification, and a formal Data Protection Officer designation. Summarized in-app as a combined "Privacy & data protection" page (linked as "Privacy" from the public landing page and signed-in app footers), downloadable as a watermarked PDF via the browser's print dialog — not only reachable by reading this repo doc.
- **[Cookie Policy](https://github.com/Abdurleone/kejaapp/blob/main/docs/compliance/cookie-policy.md)** — the actual cookies and `localStorage` keys the web frontend uses (`keja_token`, `keja_refresh`, theme preference, dev API override) and why none currently require a consent banner.
- **[Records of Processing Activities](https://github.com/Abdurleone/kejaapp/blob/main/docs/compliance/records-of-processing-activities.md)** — an 18-row GDPR Art. 30-style register mapping every KejaApp feature to the personal data it touches, its legal basis, recipients, and retention.
- **[Data Protection Impact Assessments](https://github.com/Abdurleone/kejaapp/blob/main/docs/compliance/data-protection-impact-assessment.md)** — risk assessments for the three highest-risk processing activities: mover request pickup geolocation, property image fingerprinting, and admin account moderation.

## Security and operations

- **[Incident Response Plan](https://github.com/Abdurleone/kejaapp/blob/main/docs/compliance/incident-response-plan.md)** — the internal runbook for *handling* a security/data incident (severity levels, containment, ODPC/user notification, post-incident review) — distinct from [`SECURITY.md`](https://github.com/Abdurleone/kejaapp/blob/main/docs/SECURITY.md), which covers how an outsider *reports* a vulnerability.
- **[Database Access Policy](https://github.com/Abdurleone/kejaapp/blob/main/docs/compliance/database-access-policy.md)** — how access to MongoDB is actually controlled: connection-level security, data classification, the application-layer ownership/role-based scoping that plays the role a database-engine access-control layer would (no client ever holds a database credential), and honestly-flagged gaps (unverified Atlas user privilege, backups still manual not automated).
- **[Records Retention & Protection Policy](https://github.com/Abdurleone/kejaapp/blob/main/docs/compliance/records-retention-and-protection-policy.md)** — how KejaApp's own operational records (audit logs, moderation/violation records, incident documentation) are protected and retained — distinct from the user-facing personal-data lifecycle in the Data Protection Policy above.
- **[ISO/IEC 27001 Statement of Applicability](https://github.com/Abdurleone/kejaapp/blob/main/docs/compliance/iso27001-statement-of-applicability.md)** — a self-assessment against all 93 Annex A:2022 controls (organizational, people, physical, technological), honestly marking each as implemented, partial, or a genuine gap — including a phased remediation roadmap, kept current in that document rather than summarized here (its own priorities shift as items close out).
- **[SOC 2 Readiness Assessment](https://github.com/Abdurleone/kejaapp/blob/main/docs/compliance/soc2-readiness-assessment.md)** — crosswalks the ISO 27001 self-assessment onto SOC 2's Trust Services Criteria (Security, Availability, Confidentiality) so the existing controls translate directly whenever a real audit is pursued. Explicitly **not** a SOC 2 report — that requires an independent CPA firm engagement, which hasn't happened.
- **[Risk Register](https://github.com/Abdurleone/kejaapp/blob/main/docs/compliance/risk.md)** — a single prioritized list ranking risk across security, availability, data integrity, supply chain, product/business scope, and organizational factors together, cross-referencing the SoA/SOC 2/database-access-policy documents above rather than repeating their control-level detail. Top-ranked items: CI being disabled since 2026-07-06, the single-instance production deployment, and manual (not scheduled) database backups.
- **[Accessibility Statement](https://github.com/Abdurleone/kejaapp/blob/main/docs/compliance/accessibility-statement.md)** — what's implemented against WCAG 2.1 AA today (touch targets, reduced-motion support, keyboard-accessible admin table, the auth modal's dialog semantics/focus trap, the main nav's ARIA tablist pattern, alt text) versus what hasn't been audited yet, and how that target maps onto Kenya's own Persons with Disabilities Act, 2025.
- **[EU AI Act Applicability Assessment](https://github.com/Abdurleone/kejaapp/blob/main/docs/compliance/eu-ai-act-applicability-assessment.md)** — a self-assessment of whether the EU Artificial Intelligence Act applies to KejaApp: every "smart" feature (duplicate-image detection, saved-search matching, account lockout) is deterministic, human-authored logic with no inference or learning involved, so no AI system exists under the Act's own definition today — with a specific flag for what would change that (an ML feature, or automated tenant screening/scoring in particular).
