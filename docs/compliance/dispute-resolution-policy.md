# JakezApp Dispute Resolution & Complaints Policy

## 1. Two different kinds of disputes

JakezApp draws a firm line between two categories of disagreement, because it has a different role in each:

1. **Disputes between users** — a tenant and a landlord/agency disagreeing about a viewing, a deposit, a listing's accuracy, or the outcome of a review; a tenant and a mover disagreeing about a moving job. JakezApp is **not a party** to these relationships (see [Terms of Service §2](terms-of-service.md#2-what-jakezapp-is--and-isnt) and the [Payment Boundary](../../README.md#payment-boundary)) and does not arbitrate, mediate, or decide who's right.
2. **Complaints about JakezApp itself, or about how it enforced its own rules** — an account suspension you believe was wrong, a violation you believe was a false positive, a data-protection request that wasn't handled properly, or a bug affecting your experience. JakezApp **is** directly responsible for these and commits to a defined process below.

## 2. Disputes between users

What JakezApp provides:

- The platform tools to resolve it directly — an inquiry thread, a review-and-response mechanism, and each party's contact details, so a tenant and owner (or tenant and mover) can work it out between themselves.
- A place to **report** conduct that violates the [Acceptable Use Policy](acceptable-use-policy.md) (harassment, fraud, discriminatory treatment) via the Feedback tab, which JakezApp will review as a policy-enforcement matter — not as a referee of the underlying disagreement (e.g. whether a deposit should be refunded).

What JakezApp does not provide:

- Mediation, arbitration, or a ruling on a financial or contractual disagreement between two users.
- Refunds, compensation, or enforcement of any payment arrangement — those are entirely between the parties who made them (see the Payment Boundary).

For a dispute that can't be resolved directly and involves a genuine legal claim, ordinary legal remedies available under Kenyan law (e.g. small claims court for a rent-deposit dispute) remain available to the parties involved, independent of JakezApp.

## 3. Complaints about JakezApp or its enforcement decisions

### 3.1 How to complain

Use the in-app **Feedback tab** for most complaints. For anything involving personal data or account security specifically, email `privacy@jakezapp.com` instead — see the [Data Protection Policy](data-protection-policy.md#10-data-subject-rights).

### 3.2 What happens next

1. An admin reviews the submission.
2. For an **enforcement appeal** (a suspension, ban, or violation you believe was wrong): the admin re-examines the underlying record — e.g. the specific image-duplication match, or the reported conduct — and either reverses the action (with the account/status corrected) or explains why it stands.
3. For a **data-protection complaint**: handled per the process in the [Data Protection Policy §10](data-protection-policy.md#10-data-subject-rights), with a one-month target for a substantive response.
4. For a **general product complaint or bug report**: reviewed and, once responded to, closed — a response is required before any submitted feedback item is marked resolved, per the existing feedback workflow.

### 3.3 If you're not satisfied

Where a complaint concerns how JakezApp has handled your personal data specifically, you retain the right — independent of anything JakezApp itself decides — to lodge a complaint directly with the **Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC)** in Kenya, or with the relevant supervisory authority in another jurisdiction where GDPR-equivalent protections apply to you.

## 4. Enforcement transparency

Every account status change JakezApp's admins make is logged with a reason and visible to the affected user (see [Code of Ethics §3](code-of-ethics.md#3-enforcement) and the [Admin Manual](../user-manual/admin-manual.md#2-managing-user-accounts)) — so a complaint about an enforcement decision always starts from a record both sides can see, not a black box.

## 5. Contact

- General complaints/feedback: in-app Feedback tab.
- Data protection complaints: `privacy@jakezapp.com`.
- Security vulnerabilities: [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md).
