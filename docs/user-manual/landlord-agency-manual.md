# KejaApp User Manual — Landlords & Agencies

See the [General Guide](general-manual.md) first for account creation, sign-in, notifications, and data/privacy basics common to every role.

Landlords and agencies share the same set of listing-management capabilities; agencies additionally have access to the verification workflow described in [Section 5](#5-agency-verification).

## 1. Your workspace, not the general Discover list

Unlike tenants, landlords and agencies do not browse the general Discover list — your home base is the **Workspace** tab, showing only the properties you own. If you follow a direct link to another owner's property detail page, you'll only see full details for listings you own; other owners' listings show a message directing you back to your own Workspace instead. This keeps every owner's attention on their own inventory and their own tenant interactions.

## 2. Creating and managing listings

From Workspace:

- **New listing** — set title, description, location (with coordinates for radius search to work), rent/deposit/agency fee, amenities, images, lifecycle status, viewing type (`scheduled` or `open`), and listing contact details (preferred method, phone/email/WhatsApp, hours, notes).
- **Edit** — update any of the above on an existing listing you own.
- **Lifecycle status** — `draft` (not public), `available` (shown to tenants), `taken`, or `archived` (both hidden from the public list). Only `available` listings accept new inquiries/viewing requests.
- **Images** — add image URLs (or upload directly) with optional alt text; remove images you no longer want shown.

Pricing fields are landlord/agency-only — tenants and admins cannot set or edit them.

## 3. Responding to tenant activity

- **Inquiries** — see every inquiry sent about any of your properties in one place (Workspace), scoped automatically to properties you own. Respond to move an inquiry from `open` to `responded`; the tenant is notified either way.
- **Viewing requests** — see every viewing request sent about any of your properties in one place (Workspace's "Viewing requests" panel), scoped automatically to properties you own, and approve, reject, cancel, or complete each directly. The requester is notified on every status change. A **scheduled**-viewing listing requires the tenant to pick a future date; an **open**-viewing listing auto-approves without one.
- **Reviews** — you can respond publicly to a review on your property, but you cannot delete a review or its rating, even if you disagree with it.

## 4. Mover affiliates

If you work with movers you trust, mark them as **affiliates** from Movers (its own tab on web; on mobile, via **More → Movers**) (add/remove). An affiliated mover appears under **"Recommended by the owner"** on every one of your properties' detail pages, ahead of the general "movers nearby" list — a way to vouch for movers you've had a good experience with, without KejaApp itself endorsing them.

## 5. Agency verification

Agencies (not landlords) can submit a verification request: business name, registration number, business contact details, and supporting documents. An admin reviews it and approves or rejects (with a reason if rejected). Approval adds a **"Verified agency"** badge next to your name on every listing, visible to tenants on Discover cards and property detail — a trust signal, not a requirement to keep operating. Your listings work identically whether or not you're verified; the badge simply doesn't appear until approval.

Check your current status any time from the Dashboard.

## 6. What landlords/agencies can't do

- Browse or open other owners' full property listings (see Section 1).
- Moderate user accounts, review agency/mover verification decisions, or review platform-wide violations — those are admin capabilities.
- Process rent, deposit, or agency-fee payments through KejaApp itself — that remains a direct arrangement with the tenant.

Your listings and conduct are governed by the [Terms of Service](../terms-of-service.md) and [Acceptable Use Policy](../acceptable-use-policy.md) — misrepresenting a listing, discriminatory terms, and reused listing images are all grounds for account action. A disagreement with a specific tenant isn't mediated by KejaApp directly — see the [Dispute Resolution & Complaints Policy](../dispute-resolution-policy.md).

## 7. Your data as a business account

Verification documents and business details you submit are visible only to admins reviewing your request and to you. Tenant contact details you receive through an inquiry or viewing request are provided so you can respond to that specific tenant — see the [Data Protection Policy](../data-protection-policy.md) for how that data is scoped and protected.

## 8. Deleting your account

See the [General Guide §6](general-manual.md#6-your-data-and-privacy) for how to actually do it. As a landlord/agency, this removes your profile, your listings, inquiries/viewing requests/reviews tied to those listings, agency verification records, and notifications. See the [Data Protection Policy](../data-protection-policy.md#9-retention-and-deletion) for the exact scope.
