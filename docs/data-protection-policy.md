# KejaApp Data Protection Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes what personal data KejaApp collects, why, how it is protected, how long it is kept, and what rights a user has over their own data. It is written to align with:

- The **Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019** ("the Act") and the regulations made under it, enforced by the **Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC)**.
- The **EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**, as a widely referenced international baseline for the same principles.
- The control themes of **ISO/IEC 27001** (information security management), **ISO/IEC 27701** (privacy information management), and **ISO/IEC 29100** (privacy framework terminology).

This is operational, product-level documentation of KejaApp's actual data handling — see [Section 14](#14-scope-and-limitations) for what this policy does and does not substitute for.

This policy is one of a related set: the [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.md) governs your relationship with KejaApp generally; the [Cookie Policy](cookie-policy.md) covers browser cookies and local storage specifically; the [Records of Processing Activities](records-of-processing-activities.md) is the granular, tabular version of [Section 3](#3-data-we-collect) below; and the [Data Protection Impact Assessments](data-protection-impact-assessment.md) document the risk analysis behind the higher-risk activities referenced throughout.

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Personal data | Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person (name, email, phone, location, etc.) — matches the Act's definition and GDPR Art. 4(1). |
| Sensitive personal data | Under the Act, includes data revealing family details, marital status, health, race/ethnicity, and similar categories. KejaApp does not intentionally collect sensitive personal data as defined by the Act — see [Section 4](#4-what-we-dont-collect). |
| Data controller | The entity that decides why and how personal data is processed. KejaApp is the data controller for all data described in this policy. |
| Data processor | A third party that processes data on the controller's behalf under instruction. See [Section 7](#7-third-party-processors) for KejaApp's processors. |
| Data subject | The individual the personal data is about — i.e., a KejaApp user. |

## 3. Data we collect

Collected directly from users, or generated by their use of the platform:

| Category | Fields | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Account data | Name, email, username, phone number, password (hashed, never stored in plain text), role (tenant/landlord/agency/mover/admin), account status | Registration, profile updates |
| Session data | Hashed refresh tokens, auth cookies, registered mobile device push tokens | Login, mobile app registration |
| Location data | Property/mover business coordinates (landlord, agency, mover); a tenant's device coordinates captured only at the moment of submitting a mover service request (pickup point) | Listing/profile creation; one-time device geolocation prompt |
| Listing data | Property details, pricing, amenities, images, listing contact details (submitted by landlords/agencies) | Property creation/editing |
| Transaction/interaction data | Inquiries, viewing requests, mover service requests, saved favorites, saved searches, reviews, ratings, platform feedback | Normal use of the respective feature |
| Verification data | Agency/mover business name, registration number, business contact details, and submitted supporting documents | Agency/mover verification submission |
| Notifications | In-app notification content and read/unread state | Generated by the events above |
| Moderation data | Violation records (e.g. duplicate-listing-image detection), account status change history and reasons | Automated trust-and-safety checks, admin review |

KejaApp does not require any of this data from a visitor who is only browsing available listings anonymously — account data is only collected at registration.

## 4. What we don't collect

KejaApp does not ask for or store: national ID/passport numbers, financial account or card details (see [Payment Boundary](../README.md#payment-boundary) — no payment is processed on-platform), health information, or any of the sensitive personal data categories defined by the Act, beyond what a user might voluntarily and unnecessarily include in free-text fields (e.g. an inquiry message) — users are advised not to include such information in free-text fields, and it is not solicited by any form field in the app.

## 5. Purpose and legal basis for processing

Each category above is processed for a specific, disclosed purpose — consistent with the Act's principle that data be collected for "specified, explicit and legitimate purposes" (mirrors GDPR Art. 5(1)(b)):

- **Account and session data** — to authenticate the user and maintain their session (legal basis: performance of a contract — the user agrees to these terms by registering).
- **Location data** — to power radius-based property/mover search and to compute a pickup-to-dropoff distance for a mover request the tenant chose to submit (legal basis: consent — captured via a device permission prompt; the app functions with the feature simply omitted if permission is declined or unavailable).
- **Listing, transaction, and interaction data** — to operate the core rental-discovery and moving-coordination features the user signed up for (legal basis: contract performance).
- **Verification data** — to assess and display a trust signal for agencies and movers (legal basis: legitimate interest in platform trust and safety, and consent from the submitting business).
- **Moderation data** — to detect and act on fraud/abuse (legal basis: legitimate interest in protecting other users from fraudulent listings).

## 6. Data minimization, accuracy, and storage limitation

- Only fields required for a feature to function are collected — for example, a mover's pickup location is computed at request time and attached to the API response, never persisted to the database, so it cannot go stale or need separate deletion.
- Users can correct their own account data (name, phone) at any time via the Account page; email, username, and role are immutable after registration and require contacting `privacy@kejaapp.com` to change.
- Data is retained only as long as the account is active or as required by [Section 9](#9-retention-and-deletion).

## 7. Third-party processors

| Processor | Purpose | Data involved |
|---|---|---|
| MongoDB (self-hosted or Atlas, depending on deployment) | Primary data store | All categories above |
| S3-compatible object storage (when `STORAGE_DRIVER` is set to a bucket backend) | Property listing image storage | Uploaded image files only |
| Expo push notification service | Delivering mobile push notifications | Device push token, notification title/body |
| Redis (when `REDIS_URL` is set) | Rate limiting and response caching | Transient, non-personal request-count data; cached public listing data |

KejaApp does not sell personal data to any third party, and does not share personal data with advertisers.

## 8. International data transfers

Where a processor above operates infrastructure outside Kenya, KejaApp relies on that processor's own data-protection safeguards (encryption in transit, provider-level compliance commitments) as the basis for transfer, consistent with the Act's requirement (s. 48) that a cross-border transfer have appropriate safeguards, and with GDPR's equivalent transfer-mechanism requirements (Art. 44–49). Whoever operates a given KejaApp deployment is responsible for confirming their chosen hosting providers meet this bar for their jurisdiction — see [`docs/devops.md`](devops.md) for deployment-specific infrastructure choices.

## 9. Retention and deletion

- **Account deletion**: a signed-in user can delete their own account from the Account page (type `DELETE` to confirm) or via the public [Delete Account](../frontend/src/pages/DeleteAccountPage.jsx) page if they can't sign in, which emails `privacy@kejaapp.com`. Deletion is immediate and removes: the account itself, sessions, saved favorites, inquiries, viewing requests, reviews, notifications, agency verification records, image-fingerprint records, violation and status-change history, every property the user owns, mover business profile and verification records, mover service requests (sent or received), platform feedback submissions, saved searches, and registered device push tokens. Where the user affiliated movers as a landlord/agency, or was affiliated as a mover, that link is also removed from the other party's records.
- **Inactive accounts**: KejaApp does not currently auto-delete accounts purely for inactivity; deletion is user-initiated or admin-initiated (e.g. following a ban, on request).

## 10. Data subject rights

Consistent with the Act (s. 26) and GDPR (Art. 15–21), every user has the right to:

| Right | How to exercise it today |
|---|---|
| Be informed | This policy, plus the in-app [Privacy & data protection page](../frontend/src/pages/DataProtectionPage.jsx), downloadable as a PDF from that page |
| Access their data | `GET /api/auth/me`, and every list view (Saved, Workspace, Notifications, etc.) already shows a user their own records; a full data export beyond what's visible in-app can be requested from `privacy@kejaapp.com` |
| Correct their data | Account page (name, phone); email `privacy@kejaapp.com` for fields not user-editable |
| Delete their data | Account page or Delete Account page — see [Section 9](#9-retention-and-deletion) |
| Object to / restrict processing | Email `privacy@kejaapp.com` — e.g. to opt out of push notifications, remove device tokens via the app's own notification settings |
| Data portability | Email `privacy@kejaapp.com` for a machine-readable export of account data |

Requests sent to `privacy@kejaapp.com` should be acknowledged promptly; the Act does not fix a statutory response window for controller-handled requests the way GDPR's one-month default does, but a one-month target is a reasonable operating benchmark to hold this policy to.

## 11. Security measures

Aligned with ISO/IEC 27001 Annex A control themes (access control, cryptography, operations security, incident management):

- Passwords are hashed (never stored or logged in plain text); refresh tokens are stored as hashes, not raw values.
- JWT-based authentication with HTTP-only auth cookies as an alternative to bearer tokens; role-based authorization enforced on every protected endpoint.
- HTTPS is required in production deployments (see [Deployment](https://github.com/Abdurleone/kejaapp/wiki/Deployment)) so data is encrypted in transit.
- Configurable rate limiting (Redis-backed in production) mitigates credential-stuffing and scraping.
- Centralized request validation and error handling reduce the risk of malformed input reaching the data layer.
- Admin actions that change account status are logged with an actor, reason, and timestamp, forming an audit trail (see [Code of Ethics §2.6](code-of-ethics.md#26-conflict-of-interest) on how that audit trail is used).
- Uploaded property images are fingerprinted to detect and flag duplicate/fraudulent reuse across accounts.

## 12. Breach notification

In the event of a personal data breach, KejaApp's operating team commits to:

1. Assess the breach and contain it as soon as it is discovered.
2. Notify the ODPC within 72 hours of becoming aware, where the breach is likely to result in a risk to data subjects — mirroring both the Act's expectations and GDPR Art. 33's 72-hour benchmark.
3. Notify affected users directly, without undue delay, where the breach is likely to result in a high risk to their rights and freedoms (GDPR Art. 34 / Act equivalent).
4. Document the breach, its cause, and the remediation taken.

## 13. Children's data

KejaApp is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. Account registration assumes an adult user entering into a rental or moving-service relationship.

## 14. Scope and limitations

This policy documents KejaApp's actual current data-handling practices, in language chosen to track the Act's principles, GDPR's equivalent articles, and ISO privacy/security control themes, so that whoever operates or audits a KejaApp deployment can quickly map real behavior to those frameworks. It is:

- **Not** a substitute for formal ODPC registration (required of most data controllers/processors under the Act's registration regulations) — confirm registration status and any applicable exemption threshold with the ODPC directly for a given legal entity operating KejaApp.
- **Not** a substitute for review by a qualified data-protection or legal professional before relying on it for real-world compliance.
- **Not** a claim of ISO/IEC 27001 or 27701 certification — no external audit against those standards has been performed.

## 15. Data Protection Officer

KejaApp designates a **Data Protection Officer (DPO) function** — the role, not necessarily a dedicated full-time position — responsible for:

- Monitoring compliance with this policy, the [Records of Processing Activities](records-of-processing-activities.md), and the [Data Protection Impact Assessments](data-protection-impact-assessment.md).
- Acting as the point of contact for the ODPC, for data subjects, and for internal questions about a new feature's data-protection implications before it ships.
- Reviewing and signing off on rights requests, breach notifications (per [Section 12](#12-breach-notification)), and this policy's periodic review (per [Section 16](#16-review)).

Whoever operates a given KejaApp deployment as its legal data controller is responsible for formally naming an individual to this function — required under the Act for certain categories of controller/processor — and confirming with the ODPC whether their specific entity meets that threshold. Until formally named, `privacy@kejaapp.com` is the designated contact channel through which this function is exercised.
